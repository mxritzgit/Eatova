# Training artwork

`nightstudio.png` is original decorative equipment photography generated with
the image-generation tool on 2026-09-13 for the selected Nachtstudio concept 09.
It is bundled for offline use and does not depict or identify a user's exercise.
No external image service is contacted at runtime.

The brief: matte black weight plate on a rubber studio floor, restrained
lavender side light, equipment to the right and dark negative space to the left;
no people, logos, text or UI. The generated asset is used unchanged.
